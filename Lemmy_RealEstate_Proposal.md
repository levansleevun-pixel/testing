## 1. Website Audit & Recommendations

### Current Issues Identified
- Slow page load times (negatively impacts both user experience and search rankings)
- Non-optimized mobile experience — majority of real estate searches happen on mobile
- Weak calls-to-action (CTAs) — visitors don't know what to do next
- No live chat or instant contact widget
- Property listings lack rich detail, photography guidance, and schema markup
- No lead capture forms tied to specific listings
- Missing trust signals: testimonials, awards, agent bios are buried or absent

### Recommendations

| Priority | Improvement | Impact |
|----------|-------------|--------|
| High | Mobile-first redesign / responsive optimization | More leads from mobile users |
| High | Speed optimization (Core Web Vitals fix) | Better rankings + lower bounce rate |
| High | Add prominent CTAs on every page ("Schedule a Viewing", "Get a Free Valuation") | More conversions |
| Medium | Live chat / chatbot integration (e.g., Tidio, Drift) | Faster lead response time |
| Medium | Dedicated neighborhood/area landing pages | Local SEO boost |
| Medium | Agent profile pages with reviews and contact forms | Trust + direct lead capture |
| Low | Interactive mortgage calculator | Increased time-on-site |
| Low | Virtual tour / 3D walkthrough integration | Higher listing engagement |

## 2. SEO — Search Engine Optimization

### Current State
- Limited keyword targeting — missing high-intent local search terms
- No structured data (schema markup) on listings
- Weak internal linking structure
- Thin content — no blog, no guides, no local resource pages
- Few quality backlinks

### Strategy

#### Keyword Targeting
Focus on high-intent, local keywords such as:
- "Homes for sale in [City/Neighborhood]"
- "Real estate agent in [City]"
- "Buy/sell property in [City]"
- "[City] luxury homes"
- "First-time home buyer [City]"

#### On-Page SEO
- Optimize all title tags, meta descriptions, and header tags
- Add **schema markup** (RealEstateListing, LocalBusiness, Review) to improve rich snippets in Google
- Create unique, keyword-rich descriptions for every listing
- Fix duplicate content issues across similar listing pages

#### Content Marketing (Blog Strategy)
- Monthly blog posts targeting local buyers/sellers:
  - "Best Neighborhoods to Buy in [City] in 2026"
  - "How to Sell Your Home Fast in [City]"
  - "First-Time Buyer Guide for [City]"
- Build topical authority as the go-to real estate resource locally

#### Local SEO
- Fully optimize **Google Business Profile** (photos, posts, Q&A, reviews)
- Build consistent citations on Zillow, Realtor.com, Yelp, Bing Places
- Earn local backlinks from community blogs, news sites, and partner businesses
- Encourage satisfied clients to leave Google reviews (systematic follow-up process)

#### Technical SEO
- Submit updated XML sitemap
- Fix crawl errors and broken links
- Ensure all pages are indexed properly
- Implement HTTPS site-wide if not already done

## 3. SEM — Search Engine Marketing (Paid Ads)

### Objective
Drive immediate, qualified traffic to listing pages and lead capture forms through Google Ads while organic SEO builds momentum.

### Campaign Structure

#### Campaign 1: Buyer Intent
- **Target:** People actively searching to buy
- **Keywords:** "homes for sale [city]", "3 bedroom house [city]", "[neighborhood] real estate"
- **Ad Copy Focus:** Available listings, competitive pricing, easy scheduling
- **Landing Page:** Filtered listings page with lead form

#### Campaign 2: Seller Intent
- **Target:** Homeowners looking to sell
- **Keywords:** "sell my home [city]", "home valuation [city]", "real estate agent [city]"
- **Ad Copy Focus:** Free home valuation, fast sale, expert local agents
- **Landing Page:** Free valuation landing page

#### Campaign 3: Retargeting
- **Target:** Past website visitors who didn't convert
- **Format:** Display ads + Google responsive ads
- **Goal:** Bring warm leads back to complete their inquiry

### Budget Recommendation

| Campaign | Monthly Budget | Expected Leads | Est. Cost Per Lead |
|----------|---------------|----------------|-------------------|
| Buyer Intent | $1,500 | 30–50 | $30–$50 |
| Seller Intent | $1,000 | 15–25 | $40–$67 |
| Retargeting | $500 | 10–20 | $25–$50 |
| **Total** | **$3,000** | **55–95** | **~$35–$55** |

> *Budgets are scalable. We recommend starting here and optimizing based on 30-day performance data.*

### Ongoing Management
- Weekly performance reviews
- A/B testing of ad copy and landing pages
- Negative keyword list management to eliminate wasted spend
- Monthly reporting with clear ROI breakdown

## 4. Social Media Strategy

### Current State
- Inconsistent posting schedule
- Content lacks local personality and community connection
- Low engagement — posts feel promotional, not conversational
- No paid social campaigns running
- Stories and Reels underutilized

### Platform Focus

| Platform | Priority | Why |
|----------|----------|-----|
| Instagram | High | Visual-first, ideal for listings, lifestyle, and local content |
| Facebook | High | Older buyer/seller demographic, groups, and local community |
| LinkedIn | Medium | Agent credibility, B2B referrals, investor audience |
| TikTok | Medium | Growing real estate audience, great for neighborhood tours |
| YouTube | Low (future) | Long-form neighborhood/property video content |

### Content Strategy

#### Content Pillars (3-5 posts/week)
1. **Listings** — Feature properties with high-quality photos/video (2x/week)
2. **Local Community** — Neighborhood spotlights, local events, hidden gems (1x/week)
3. **Education** — Buying/selling tips, market updates, mortgage info (1x/week)
4. **Social Proof** — Client testimonials, success stories, before/after (1x/week)
5. **Behind the Scenes** — Agent spotlights, office culture, open house previews (as available)

#### Content Formats
- **Reels/TikToks:** Property walkthroughs, neighborhood tours, quick tips
- **Carousels:** "5 Things to Know Before Buying in [City]", listing highlights
- **Stories:** Open house reminders, polls, Q&A, market stats
- **Static Posts:** Testimonials, sold listings, community highlights

### Paid Social Campaigns
- **Listing Boost Ads:** Promote new listings to local audiences (Instagram + Facebook)
- **Lead Gen Ads:** Facebook Lead Forms for "Free Home Valuation" — no website visit needed
- **Retargeting Ads:** Re-engage website visitors with listing ads on social
- **Lookalike Audiences:** Target users similar to past clients

### Community Management
- Respond to all comments and DMs within 2 business hours
- Engage in local Facebook Groups (not just posting — genuine participation)
- Monthly giveaway or contest to grow following organically
